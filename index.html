02About

Editing is rhythm, not just cuts.

I'm Ahmed, a video editor based in Cairo. For the past six years I've been turning raw footage into pieces that hold attention — from 15-second hooks to 20-minute interviews.

My process is simple: understand what the video must achieve, build the story in the timeline, then polish every frame until the edit feels invisible. Fast turnarounds, clear communication, no drama.

  • ✦ Story-first structure, retention-driven pacing
  • ✦ Sound design and color included, not an upsell
  • ✦ Built for every ratio: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5

03Services

What I can take off your plate.

From a single reel to a full post-production pipeline.

Video editing & post

Narrative cuts, pacing, transitions and a clean finish for ads, films and YouTube.

  • Story structure & rough cut
  • Titles, subtitles, lower thirds

  • Music licensing-friendly mixes

04Process

From brief to final export.

  1. 01

    Brief & footage

    You send the files, references and the goal. I confirm scope, timeline and price in writing.

  2. 02

    Assembly cut

    Story first. You get a rough cut to approve before any polish, so we never build on the wrong edit.

  3. 03

    Polish & motion

    Grade, sound design, graphics and captions. This is where the video starts to feel expensive.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Final files in every ratio, two revision rounds included, project files archived for a year.
